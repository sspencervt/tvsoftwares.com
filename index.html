About us

A home for focused software products

Timberline Ventures is a software holding company. Each of our applications has its own product, its own users and its own identity. Timberline is the shared base underneath them: it handles ownership, operations and long-term direction.

We'd rather build a few things well than many things quickly. That means listening to the people who use our products, writing careful software and running a sustainable business.

Our approach

How we build

Every product in the Timberline portfolio follows the same few principles.

Built to last

We favor reliable, maintainable software over hype. Our products are meant to stay useful for years.

Focused on users

Each product solves a specific problem for specific people, and we keep improving it based on what they tell us.

Privacy by default

We collect only what a product needs to work, and we're open about how that data is handled.

Sustainable growth

We grow carefully, adding products and features when they make sense and not just to chase a trend.

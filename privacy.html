Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Timberline Ventures LLC ("Timberline," "we," "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our mobile and desktop applications (the "Apps") or visit this website (together, the "Services"). It applies to every App published by Timberline Ventures LLC, unless an App links to its own privacy policy. In that case, the App's own policy controls for that App.

The short version: we collect only what an App needs to work, we don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your data for third-party advertising.

Information we collect

What we collect depends on which App you use and which features you turn on. Each App's listing on the App Store includes an "App Privacy" section that shows the data types that App collects.

  • Information you provide. If an App offers an account, we collect what you give us to create it, such as your name and email address. If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Content you create. Files, notes, settings or other content you create in an App. Where possible this stays on your device or in your own iCloud account. If an App syncs or backs up content to our servers, we store it only so we can provide that feature to you.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We don't receive or store your payment card details. Apple tells us about the purchase, such as the product and a transaction ID, so we can unlock what you paid for.
  • Diagnostics and usage data. Apps may collect crash reports, performance data and basic, non-identifying usage information so we can fix bugs and improve the App. If you've agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may also provide us with aggregated crash and usage data.
  • Website data. This website has no accounts, forms or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and operations.

We don't collect precise location, contacts, photos, health data or other sensitive information unless an App clearly needs it for a feature you choose to use. In that case, the App asks for your permission first, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services.
  • To process purchases and restore them on your devices.
  • To respond to support requests and other messages.
  • To diagnose problems, prevent abuse and keep the Services secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Tracking and advertising

We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't track you across other companies' apps and websites. If an App ever needs to do so, it will ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt first.

How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the Services, such as Apple (App Store, purchases and iCloud), cloud hosting, email and crash reporting providers. They may use the information only to provide services to us, and they must protect it at least as well as this policy requires.
  • Legal reasons. When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Timberline, our users or others.
  • Business transfers. If Timberline or one of its Apps is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner, who must continue to honor this policy.

Data retention and deletion

We keep personal information only as long as we need it to provide the Services, or as the law requires. Support emails are kept for normal business recordkeeping.

If an App lets you create an account, you can delete your account and its associated data from within the App. You can also ask us to delete it by emailing the address below. We'll complete deletion requests within 30 days, except for information we must keep by law. Content stored only on your device or in your iCloud account is deleted when you delete the App or remove it from iCloud.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and fix issues quickly.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including California, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also withdraw any permission you've given at any time in your device settings. To make a request, email us at the address below. We won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International users

Timberline is based in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States or wherever our service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the App or by other reasonable means.
